# Networking Email Scripts

Short, warm scripts for reaching out during a job search. Fill in the
bracketed parts. Print the matching page at `printable/networking-emails.html`.

## 1. Reaching out to someone you know

**Subject:** Catching up / quick question about [Company]

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well. It's been a while since we [worked together / met at
event / were in school together].

I'm applying for the [Role] position at [Company], and I remembered you [work
there / know someone there]. Would you be open to a brief chat about what it's
like to work there and any advice for applying?

No pressure at all if you're busy. Either way, I'd love to catch up.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

---

## 2. Asking for a referral

**Subject:** Quick favor — referral for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [Name],

I submitted an application for the [Role] position at [Company] (Job ID
[####], if you have it). I think my experience in [skill/area] would be a good
fit.

If you're comfortable, would you mind referring me internally? I've attached
my resume and the job description to make it easy.

Totally understand if you'd rather not — no hard feelings either way.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

---

## 3. Following up after applying

**Subject:** Following up — [Role] application — [Your Name]

Hi [Hiring Manager Name or Hiring Team],

I applied for the [Role] position on [Date] and remain very interested. I'd
welcome the chance to talk about how my [skill/experience] could help [Company].

Please let me know if there's anything else I can send over.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

*Send this about one week after applying, unless the posting says otherwise.*

---

## 4. Thank-you after an interview

**Subject:** Thank you — [Role] interview — [Your Name]

Hi [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for speaking with me today about the [Role] position. I enjoyed
learning about [specific thing you discussed], and it made me even more
excited about the possibility of joining [Company].

Our conversation about [topic] reinforced that my experience in [skill] would
let me contribute quickly. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need
anything else from me.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

*Send within 24 hours of the interview.*

---

## Tips

- Keep emails short. People skim on their phones.
- One clear ask per email.
- Proofread names — spell the company and the person's name correctly.
- Track each email in your application tracker (`kit/application-tracker.md`).
