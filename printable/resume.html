Resume Template

A plain-English resume template and step-by-step guide. Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and fill in your details.

Before you write: keep it simple

  • One page if you can, two at most.
  • Plain fonts (Arial, Calibri, or Georgia). 10–12 point body text.
  • Save as PDF when you send it. Name the file: FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf.
  • No photos, no age, no marital status, no Social Security number. Keep it professional and safe.

Quick checklist

  • Name and contact info at the top
  • Most recent job first
  • Each bullet starts with an action verb (Managed, Created, Trained, Reduced…)
  • Numbers included where you can (how many, how much, how often)
  • No typos — read it out loud, then ask a friend to read it too
  • Saved as PDF before sending
