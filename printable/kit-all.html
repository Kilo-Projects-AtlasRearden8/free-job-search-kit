Cover Letter Template

One page, three or four short paragraphs. Connect their needs to your experience.

[Your Full Name]
[City, State] | [Phone Number] | [Email Address]
[Date]

[Hiring Manager Name]
[Hiring Manager Title]
[Company Name]
[Company City, State]

Dear [Hiring Manager Name or Hiring Manager],

**Opening paragraph.** Which job you're applying for, where you saw it, and why you're interested.

**Body paragraph 1.** Your most relevant experience, with one short example and a result.

**Body paragraph 2.** One more relevant strength — skills, license, certification, or a soft skill.

**Closing paragraph.** Welcome a conversation, thank them, mention how to reach you.

Sincerely,

[Your Full Name]

Interview Prep Questions

Use the STAR method for stories: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

Warm-up

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Why do you want this job?
  3. Why are you leaving your current role?

Experience and skills

  1. What's your greatest strength?
  2. What's your greatest weakness?
  3. Describe a time you handled a difficult patient or coworker.
  4. Tell me about a time you made a mistake.

Scenario questions (healthcare)

  1. What would you do if you noticed a medication error?
  2. How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?
  3. How do you explain something complicated to a patient?

Questions to ask them

  • What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?
  • Can you tell me about the team I'd be working with?
  • What's onboarding and training like for new hires?
  • What do you enjoy most about working here?
  • What are the next steps in the hiring process?

Networking Email Scripts

Short, warm scripts. Fill in the bracketed parts. One clear ask per email.

1. Reaching out to someone you know

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well. It's been a while since we [worked together / met at event / were in school together].

I'm applying for the [Role] position at [Company], and I remembered you [work there / know someone there]. Would you be open to a brief chat about what it's like to work there and any advice for applying?

No pressure at all if you're busy. Either way, I'd love to catch up.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

2. Asking for a referral

Hi [Name],

I submitted an application for the [Role] position at [Company] (Job ID [####], if you have it). I think my experience in [skill/area] would be a good fit.

If you're comfortable, would you mind referring me internally? I've attached my resume and the job description to make it easy.

Totally understand if you'd rather not — no hard feelings either way.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

3. Following up after applying

Hi [Hiring Manager Name or Hiring Team],

I applied for the [Role] position on [Date] and remain very interested. I'd welcome the chance to talk about how my [skill/experience] could help [Company].

Please let me know if there's anything else I can send over.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

Send about one week after applying, unless the posting says otherwise.

4. Thank-you after an interview

Hi [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for speaking with me today about the [Role] position. I enjoyed learning about [specific thing you discussed], and it made me even more excited about the possibility of joining [Company].

Our conversation about [topic] reinforced that my experience in [skill] would let me contribute quickly. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need anything else from me.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]

Send within 24 hours of the interview.
