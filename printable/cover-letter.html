Cover Letter Template

A plain-English cover letter template and guide. One page, three or four short paragraphs. Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and fill in your details.

Before you write

  • Keep it to one page. Three or four short paragraphs.
  • Address it to a real person when you can ([Hiring Manager] works if you can't find a name).
  • Connect their needs to your experience. Don't just repeat your resume.
  • Save as PDF: FirstName-LastName-Cover-Letter.pdf.

Template

[Your Full Name]
[City, State] | [Phone Number] | [Email Address]
[Date]

[Hiring Manager Name]
[Hiring Manager Title]
[Company Name]
[Company City, State]

Dear [Hiring Manager Name or Hiring Manager],

**Opening paragraph.** Say which job you're applying for and where you saw it.
Add one sentence about why you're interested.

**Body paragraph 1.** Connect your most relevant experience to this role. Use
one short example with a result.

**Body paragraph 2.** Add one more relevant strength (skills, license,
certification, or a soft skill like communication or reliability).

**Closing paragraph.** Say you'd welcome a conversation, thank them for their
time, and mention how to reach you.

Sincerely,

[Your Full Name]

Dear Alex Rivera,

I'm writing to apply for the Registered Nurse position at Northside Clinic,
which I saw posted on the clinic's website. I've admired Northside's focus on
same-day patient education, and I'd love to bring my med-surg experience to
your team.

For the past four years I've worked as a registered nurse on a 32-bed
telemetry unit at City General Hospital, where I cared for 5–8 patients per
shift. I built a habit of double-checking discharge instructions with each
patient, and our unit saw fewer medication-related call-backs after I started
walking every patient through their new medications before they left.

I also hold an active Indiana RN license and a current BLS/CPR certification.
I'm known for calm, clear communication with patients and families, especially
when someone is scared or in pain — something I know Northside Clinic sees
every day.

I'd welcome the chance to talk about how my experience fits your team. Thank
you for your time and consideration. You can reach me at (555) 123-4567 or
jordan.lee@email.com.

Sincerely,

Jordan Lee

Quick checklist

  • One page or less
  • Addressed to a person (not "To Whom It May Concern")
  • Names the specific job you want
  • Includes at least one specific example with a result
  • Mentions your license or certification, if the job requires one
  • Contact info matches your resume
  • Saved as PDF with a clear file name
