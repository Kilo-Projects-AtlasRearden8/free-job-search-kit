Job Application Tracker

A simple tracker to fill in as you apply. Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) or copy the table into a spreadsheet on your computer.

How to use

  • Add one row per job you apply to.
  • Update the Status column every time something changes.
  • Set a Follow-Up date so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Review it once a week.

Print extra copies or add rows as you go. No account needed — this tracker lives on your computer only.

Status ideas

  • Applied
  • Application confirmed
  • Interview scheduled (phone / video / in person)
  • Interview completed
  • Thank-you sent
  • Waiting for response
  • Offer received
  • Declined / no response
  • Withdrew application

Tips

  • Apply to a small number of jobs each week rather than 50 in one day — quality and follow-up matter more.
  • Tailor your resume and cover letter for each application when you can.
  • Save each job posting as a PDF the day you apply, in case it disappears before your interview.
